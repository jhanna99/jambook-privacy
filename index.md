# JamBook Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 22, 2026**

## Overview

JamBook is a music song sheet viewer that syncs PDF files from Dropbox. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data JamBook accesses and how it is handled.

## Data Collection

JamBook does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. There is no analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry of any kind.

## Dropbox Integration

JamBook connects to your Dropbox account to download song sheet PDF files and metadata. This connection is made directly between your device and Dropbox using OAuth2 authentication. JamBook requests read-only access to your Dropbox files and does not modify or delete any files on Dropbox.

Your Dropbox authentication tokens are stored locally on your device and are never shared with any third party.

## Local Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device:

- **PDF files** downloaded from Dropbox are cached on your device for offline access
- **User tags** you create are stored locally and are not uploaded or shared
- **App preferences** (such as your selected Dropbox folder) are stored in local device storage

## Third-Party Services

JamBook communicates only with the Dropbox API to sync your song files. No other third-party services are used.

## Data Sharing

JamBook does not share any data with third parties.

## Children's Privacy

JamBook does not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
